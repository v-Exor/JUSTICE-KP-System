# JUSTICE-KP Privacy And Retention Policy

Privacy defaults:
- VAWC, child abuse, serious injury, and referral cases are sensitive.
- Research exports should use anonymized CSV/PDF unless full identifiers are required.
- Evidence and generated forms must be downloaded only through authorized endpoints.
- Recording consent must be confirmed before operational use.

Retention defaults:
- Keep operational records according to barangay/legal guidance.
- Review transcripts, evidence, and generated forms before deletion.
- Record deletion and export events in audit logs.
